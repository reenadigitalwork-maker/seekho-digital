LEARN BY MAKING

Practical Projects

Build a connected practice portfolio around Maple Lane Home Bakery, a fictional business created for learning. Every task connects to skills from the modules.

Your connected project journey

Work through these briefs in order. Keep your drafts, explain your choices and label any sample data as simulated. You do not need a real client or paid tools to practise the planning skills.

Maple Lane Home Bakery is fictional. No real customers, sales, reviews or campaign results are being claimed.
PROJECT 01 · MODULES 1–2

Business & customer brief

Understand the business and the people it wants to help.

  • Write the business offer and service area.
  • Describe one audience by need and situation.
  • List three research questions.
  • Map a simple customer journey.

Portfolio proof: One-page business and customer brief.

PROJECT 02 · MODULE 3

Content mini-pack

Create clear messages for a weekend cake box.

  • Write three post ideas with different purposes.
  • Draft one caption and call to action.
  • Include product details without unsupported claims.

Portfolio proof: A small content calendar and copy samples.

PROJECT 03 · MODULE 4

Landing page wireframe

Plan a page that helps a visitor understand the offer.

  • Choose one main page goal.
  • Arrange headline, details, FAQs and CTA.
  • Check mobile readability and accessible labels.

Portfolio proof: Annotated page outline or wireframe.

PROJECT 04 · MODULES 5–6

Discovery & social plan

Help the right people discover useful information.

  • Choose two search phrases and explain intent.
  • Draft a page title and heading.
  • Plan three social posts for one chosen platform.

Portfolio proof: Simple SEO note and social calendar.

PROJECT 05 · MODULES 7–8

Email & campaign test plan

Plan a permission-based email and a responsible paid test.

  • Draft a welcome email for opted-in subscribers.
  • Define an objective, audience and spending limit.
  • Choose one metric and a review date.

Portfolio proof: Email draft and campaign test plan.

PROJECT 06 · MODULES 9–10

Final campaign & report

Connect the work and explain how you would learn from it.

  • Bring the brief, content and landing page together.
  • Use a small clearly labelled simulated dataset.
  • Write one insight, limitation and next action.

Portfolio proof: A short case study with honest labels.

What makes a useful portfolio sample?

Show the brief, your thinking, the work you created and how you would measure it. Separate planned outcomes from observed results. Never invent client permission, testimonials, sales or performance numbers.
