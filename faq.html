Help & FAQs

Simple answers to common questions about learning, practice and progress on Seekho Digital.

Do I need experience before starting?
No. The learning path starts with basic ideas and introduces terms as you go. Work through one lesson at a time.
Do I have to finish all modules in order?
The recommended path is Module 1 to Module 10 because the topics build on one another. You can revisit any module whenever you need.
Is Seekho Digital free?
The learning materials and practice spaces on this site are provided without a site fee. External providers may have their own paid products, certificates or terms, so check their pages before enrolling.
Will I receive a certificate or job guarantee?
No. Seekho Digital does not currently provide certificates, job placement, guaranteed employment, guaranteed business results or expert grading. The focus is on learning and creating practice work.
Are the examples real?
The Maple Lane Home Bakery examples are fictional and created for learning. They do not represent real customers, sales, reviews, ad results or client work.
How are my notes and progress saved?
They are saved in the local storage of your current browser and device. They are not sent to a teacher or stored in a cloud account. Use the workbook's download option to make a backup.
Can I use the site on my phone?
The pages are designed to adapt to mobile screens. Use the menu at the top to open learning sections. If you notice a layout problem on your device, note the page and screen size.
How should I use MCQ practice?
Try a question, check the explanation and return to the related lesson if something is unclear. The questions are practice checks, not a formal exam.
What should I put in my portfolio?
Start with a brief, customer notes, content samples, a landing page outline, a channel plan and a simple report. Clearly label simulated work and do not claim fictional work as a real client result.
What if I clear my browser data or change devices?
Local notes may be removed when browser data is cleared and they do not automatically move to another device. Download a workbook backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe.
