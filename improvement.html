Galtiyan aur improvement

Galti hona seekhne ka hissa hai. Zaroori ye hai ki aap use pakdo, theek karo aur likh lo ki aapne kya seekha.

8 steps ka improvement process

Har project ke baad isi order mein chalo.

  1. 1. Goal

    Aap kya hasil karna chahte the? Ek line mein likho.

  2. 2. Action

    Aapne kya kiya? Kaun se steps liye?

  3. 3. Evidence

    Saboot kya hai? Screenshot, link ya numbers (simulated ho to likho).

  4. 4. Problem

    Kya theek nahi tha? Saaf aur seedha likho.

  5. 5. Correction

    Aapne use kaise sudhaara?

  6. 6. Test

    Sudhaar ke baad kaise check kiya ki ab behtar hai?

  7. 7. Learning

    Aapne kya seekha jo aap agli baar yaad rakhoge?

  8. 8. Next Action

    Ab sabse pehle kya karna hai?

Atak jao to kya karein

  • Jo step nahi ho raha, use roadmap ya project mein dobara padho.
  • Pehle chhota hissa karo. Poora ek saath mat karo.
  • Apna kaam kisi dost ko dikhao aur poochho kahan samajh nahi aaya.
  • Official resource ka help ya documentation dekho.
  • Phir bhi atke ho to problem ko log mein likho aur agla hissa karo. Baad mein wapas aao.
