    Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

    Shuru karne ke liye kya aana chahiye?

    Kuch khaas nahi. Internet chalana aur ek phone ya laptop kaafi hai. Beginner Guide padho aur Level 1 se shuru karo.

    Kya Seekho Digital free hai?

    Is website ka content padhne ke liye koi payment nahi hai. Official providers ke courses, certificates ya exams par fee lag sakti hai. Ye hamesha provider ki official website par check karo.

    Kya yahan se certificate milega?

    Is release mein Seekho Digital ka apna certificate system nahi hai. Resources page par official providers ke certificates ke baare mein jaankari milegi.

    Meri progress kahan save hoti hai?

    Aapke browser ke localStorage mein, sirf isi device par. Browser data clear karne par ya doosre phone par progress nahi dikhegi.

    Kya naukri ki guarantee milti hai?

    Nahi. Hum practical skills aur portfolio banane mein madad karte hain. Naukri ya income ka koi wada nahi kiya jata.
