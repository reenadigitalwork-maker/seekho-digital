1. Digital marketing kya hai

Jab koi business internet ke zariye apne customers tak pahunchta hai, use digital marketing kehte hain. Ye Google search, social media, email, website aur online ads sab mein hota hai.

Iska asli kaam teen cheezein hain: sahi log aapko dhoondh sakein, aapki baat samajh sakein, aur phir koi action lein, jaise call, form bharna ya kharidna.

Practice ke liye ek fictional example

Maan lo "Priya Tiffin" ek kalpanik ghar ke khane ki service hai. Wo chahti hai ki office wale log use online dhoondhein. Google par "office ke liye tiffin" search karne wale ko uska page dikhe, Instagram par uske khane ki photos hon, aur WhatsApp ya form se order aaye. Ye poora process digital marketing hai.

Is guide ke saare examples fictional hain. Ye koi asli business nahi hai.

2. Digital marketing ke main types

Chhe hisse jo aapko pehle samajhne hain. Ek saath sab seekhne ki zarurat nahi.

  • SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

    Apne page ko aisa banana ki Google use sahi searches ke liye dikha sake. Isme paisa dekar ranking nahi kharidi jaati.

    Example: Priya "office tiffin service" likhne wale logon ke liye ek clear page banati hai.

  • Content marketing

    Aisa useful content (blog, video, guide) banana jo aapke customer ke sawalon ka jawab de, taaki wo aap par bharosa karne lage.

    Example: "Office mein healthy lunch kaise rakhein" naam ka blog.

  • Social media marketing

    Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn jaise platforms par regular posts se logon se judna.

    Example: Hafte mein 3 posts: menu, kitchen ki safai, customer ka sawal-jawab.

  • Email marketing

    Un logon ko email bhejna jinhone khud aapse email lene ki permission di hai.

    Example: Naye subscriber ko ek welcome email jisme is hafte ka menu ho.

  • Paid advertising

    Google ya social platforms par paisa dekar ads chalana. Aap jitna paisa lagate ho, utne logon tak pahunchte ho.

    Example: Ek chhota ad jo sirf ek sheher ke office wale logon ko dikhe.

  • Analytics

    Ye dekhna ki kitne log aaye, kya kiya aur kya kaam kar raha hai. Isse aap guess nahi, numbers ke hisaab se faisla lete ho.

    Example: Dekhna ki kaun si post se sabse zyada log website par aaye.

4. Zaroori terms, aasan bhasha mein

Keyword
Wo shabd ya sawal jo log Google mein likhte hain, jaise "office tiffin service".
Search intent
Search karne wale ka asli maqsad: jaankari chahiye, kuch kharidna hai, ya kisi site par jana hai.
Traffic
Aapki website ya page par aane wale visitors.
Impression
Aapka page ya ad kitni baar kisi ko dikha.
Click
Kitni baar kisi ne use dabaya.
CTR (Click Through Rate)
Impressions mein se kitne logon ne click kiya. Maan lo 100 ne dekha aur 5 ne click kiya, to CTR 5% hai.
CPC (Cost Per Click)
Ad par ek click ke liye aap kitna paisa dete ho.
Landing page
Wo page jahan visitor pehli baar utarta hai, aam taur par kisi ad ya link se.
CTA (Call To Action)
Wo button ya line jo visitor se kuch karne ko kehti hai, jaise "Menu dekho".
Lead
Wo vyakti jisne aapke business mein interest dikhaya, jaise form bhara ya message kiya.
Conversion
Jab visitor wo kaam kar de jo aap chahte the, jaise order ya form submit.
Funnel
Customer ka safar: pehle jaankari, phir interest, phir faisla, phir kharidari.
Buyer persona
Aapke ideal customer ka kalpanik profile: umar, kaam, dikkat aur zaroorat.
Analytics
Numbers jinse pata chalta hai ki log kya kar rahe hain.
