Two ways to reach people on a channel

A channel is where people can find a business. Organic and paid describe two different ways a business can get attention on that same channel.

LEARN

One channel, two approaches

A A channel is a place or platform a business uses to reach people, such as Instagram, Google Search or email. is where a business reaches people. A business can use organic and paid approaches on the same channel.

● Organic approach

  1. Make a useful postMaple Lane shares a cake photo and explains what is in the box.
  2. People may see itFollowers or other people may discover it through the platform.
  3. Time and effort still matterPlanning, making content and replying take work. Reach is not guaranteed.

◆ Paid approach

  1. Choose a post to promoteMaple Lane pays the platform to distribute the post.
  2. Set the campaignChoose an objective, audience and budget.
  3. Results can varyCompetition, targeting and the ad auction affect delivery. No budget guarantees fixed reach.

The channel in both examples is Instagram. The approach changes; the channel does not.

TRY

Try it with an example

Read the example and choose the approach that best describes it.

Maple Lane shares a cake photo on Instagram without paying to promote it. What is the approach?
Maple Lane pays to promote the same post to a selected audience. Which approach is this?

CHECK

Common mistakes

Mistake: “Organic means free.”

Why it is a problem
It ignores the time, skill and tools needed to plan and make content.
How to spot it
Your plan includes no time for research, writing, design or replies.
How to improve it
Record the time and resources needed, even when there is no ad-placement fee.

Mistake: “A bigger ad budget guarantees more customers.”

Why it is a problem
Competition, audience, creative, landing page and auction conditions all affect results.
How to spot it
Your plan promises fixed views, clicks or sales from a budget alone.
How to improve it
State what you will test, which metric you will watch and when you will review it. Do not promise a result.

PROVE

Check your understanding

Explain the difference in your own words. Then compare your answer with the sample.

SIMULATED practice

Maple Lane is a fictional bakery created for learning. This example does not describe a real business or campaign result.

SIMULATED sample answer

A channel is where people can find a business, such as Instagram or Google Search. Organic means the business is not paying for placement; it still takes time and effort. Paid means the business pays for distribution on that channel. The channel can stay the same while the approach changes.

Why this is strong: it defines all three terms, gives examples and explains that organic and paid are approaches—not channels.
