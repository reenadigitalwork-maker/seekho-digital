2. Typography

Only three font files load.

  • Fraunces 600 for h1, h2 and h3 only.
  • Manrope 400 for body text and 700 for labels, buttons and bold.
  • Fallbacks: Georgia for headings; system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, Arial for body. If a download fails, text still shows.
  • Body is 17px with 1.65 line height. Lesson text stays under 680px wide.

7. Definitions, callouts and data labels

Terms are explained the first time they appear. Dotted words open a definition on tap, click, hover (desktop), or keyboard focus. Esc closes it.

An ad was shown 1,000 times. Each showing is an An impression is one time an ad or post is shown on a screen. One person can see it more than once.. We measure how many people clicked using the CTR (click-through rate) = clicks ÷ impressions × 100. Platforms may count clicks differently, so check the definition..

Key term

A channel is where you reach people. Organic and paid describe how.

Example

Instagram post to followers: organic. Promoted post on Instagram: paid.

Common mistake

Treating "organic" as a channel, or as "free". It still takes time and effort.

SIMULATED sample (Strong)

Raise weekend cake box orders1 from 12 a week (average of the last 4 weeks, SIMULATED)2 to 15 a week3 by 30 June, counting orders in the order notebook4. Review on 15 June.5

  1. Metric named. "Weekend cake box orders" says what is counted.
  2. Baseline labelled. It has a period and the SIMULATED label.
  3. Target is a small step. 15 is a test, not a promise. It is 3 above the baseline.
  4. How it is counted. Anyone can repeat the count.
  5. Review date. It gives a moment to check and adjust.

    11. Motion rules

    • Motion is used for feedback only: pressing, opening, answering, progress and one reveal per section.
    • Micro-interactions last 150 to 250ms. Reveals last up to 250ms. Only transform and opacity move.
    • Nothing loops. No scroll-jacking. No animation library.
    • If your device asks for reduced motion, every animation and transition is switched off and content just appears.
